## Assumptions (to confirm with the client)
1. **High-ceiling boundary**: 5.6 m from x = 875 px eastwards, i.e. between the island and the dining
   chairs (the client: "up to the dining table"). The drawing has a thin line there, which confirms it.
   Kitchen, hall and every other room are at 2.8 m.
2. **Stair** arrives at an upper level that is not drawn; modelled as a plain plaster landing behind the
   opening in the high wall above the kitchen.
3. **TV**: the plan places the TV stand against the east glass. A TV cannot hang on glass, so it sits on a
   freestanding walnut media wall in exactly that spot.
4. **Car size**: the plan's car icon is about 436 × 168 cm; a real G 63 is 487 × 198 cm. It is centred on the
   icon, so clearances are tight (≈ 45–80 cm), as they would be in reality.
5. Decor added per brief (plants, paintings, rugs, curtains, lights) never blocks a door, window or path.
